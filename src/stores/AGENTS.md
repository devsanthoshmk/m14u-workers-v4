# App stores

- **Catalog:** `catalogStore` owns the browser preference and dialog state. Keep catalog switches independent of playback and saved-library mutations.

- **Playback:** Keep queue identity separate from the current stream URL. Trace `playerStore.ts` through browser audio events and native snapshots before changing queue, transport, or persistence. See [Architecture](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Cancellation:** Test late success and failure after a newer selection or Clear. A stale stream result must not start playback or overwrite the current request's error or loading state.
- **Android JioSaavn Fallback (`useSaavnFallbackStore`):**
  - Keep `jiosaavn.ts` guarded against `isNative()` unless `{ allowNative: true }` is explicitly passed (`saavnFallback.ts`, `search.ts` in F3 outage mode, and `getStreamData.ts` when `useSaavnFallbackStore.getState().enabled` is true).
  - **F1 per-track fallback:** In `playerStore.ts`, call `recordYouTubeFailure` and `resolveTrackFallback` (`findConfidentSaavnMatch` strict matcher) at most once per `queueId` on Android `getStreamData` failure or native open error before playback starts (`position === 0` on an unstarted YouTube row). Reload via `loadQueue` with a fresh `generation` and `resolverKey: { provider: 'jiosaavn', trackId }` without mutating `song.id` or `favorites` (`activeFailoverMatches` + lookahead `resolveRowTrackId` preserve the effective `jiosaavn:` ID). Clear per-row failover tracking on queue clear, replacement, or row removal, and discard stale fallback completions if a newer selection or `clearQueue` intervened.
  - **F3 session circuit breaker & recovery:** `recordYouTubeFailure` opens `'jiosaavn-fallback'` after 3 distinct YouTube `trackId`s fail within 5 minutes (ignoring aborts, offline errors, and duplicate retries). While open and `enabled`, `search.ts` routes Android search and suggestions to `searchJioSaavn(..., { allowNative: true })` and triggers lazy `maybeProbeRecovery()`. Recovery probes are single-flight, skip offline/`document.hidden`, back off `3m -> 6m -> 10m`, and restore `'youtube'` mode on success. Preserve `useSaavnFallbackStore.getState()` and `m14u.fallback.*` console commands (`status`, `retryYouTube`, `setEnabled`, `reset`), and keep `AndroidFallbackNotice.tsx` purely subscribed to store state.
- **Listen Along:** Keep the stub's unavailable state explicit until a real transport and room lifecycle exist.
